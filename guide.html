Start Here — the 5-minute guide

Written for someone who has never traded anything. Read this once, then go play. Nothing here can hurt you — it's all practice money, and the ticker pages now run on REAL market days.

🤔 What IS this thing?

A practice gym for day trading. It teaches you to read candlestick charts, spot one real strategy (called The Trap & Snap), and practice on replayed market mornings — all with fake money. Think flight simulator, not real plane. You crash here for free so you never crash for real.

🗺️ Your first 10 minutes (do exactly this)

1

Tap 🃏 Flashcards → Level 0 → flip 5 cards. Tap the card to see the answer, then "Got it" or "Again." That's it. Stop after 5.

2

Go home, tap 🗣️ Lingo → tap 3 words you keep hearing (like long, short, stop loss). Each one opens a plain-English explanation with an example.

3

Back home, tap any ticker chip (like NVDA) → you land on its page for a REAL trading day. Read the 📖 Recap — it shows what happened and marks the best entry and exit of the day on the chart.

4

On that same ticker page, tap 🧮 Playground → tap the chart twice (where you'd get in, where you'd get out) → Calculate 💰. Now you know what that idea would have paid (or cost).

That's your whole first day. 10 minutes. The app is designed for small daily reps, not marathon sessions.

📈 Ticker pages — Recap, Practice, Playground

Every ticker chip on the home page opens a full page built from a real trading day (the last trading day when the app was updated — real 5-minute candles, 9:30 AM–4 PM ET). Three tabs:

  • 📖 Recap — the story of the day in plain English, with the best LONG and best SHORT trades marked on the chart (green ▲ / red ▼), and what $500 would have made on each. Study this BEFORE practicing.
  • 🎮 Practice — trade that real day yourself, one candle at a time (Next candle ▶ or spacebar). You start at 10:00 AM — the first 30 minutes draw the floor and ceiling lines. BUY or SHORT when you see a setup, CLOSE whenever you want. At 4 PM it grades you against the best trade of the day.
  • 🧮 Playground — the "what if" calculator. Tap the chart twice (entry, then exit) or use the time pickers, set your money amount, hit Calculate 💰 — see the exact dollars won or lost, and a one-line lesson.
Honest note: in Recap you see the future (it's a review). In Practice you don't (it's the test). Always recap first, then practice — that's the learning loop.

🧠 Quiz — quiz, test, and review

  • Four categories: 🎒 Basics, 🕯️ Candles, 🗣️ Lingo, 🪤 Trap & Snap.
  • Each category has 3 modes: 🧠 Quiz (instant feedback — learn mode), 📝 Test (no hints, graded at the end — your best score is saved), 🔁 Review (only questions you've missed before, until you stop missing them).
  • Your best score and mistake list for every category are saved on your device and shown on the home page progress panel.

🗣️ Lingo — the dictionary

Every trading word you keep hearing, in plain English. Type in the search box (try "short" or "wick"), tap any word to open its explanation and a real example. When you feel brave, there's a quiz button at the bottom.

🃏 Flashcards — how they work

  • Tap a card → it flips, shows the answer.
  • ✅ Got it = you knew it. 😵 Again = you didn't. Be honest — the "Again" cards become your personal weak-spot review.
  • Three levels, in order: Level 0 (what trading even is) → Level 1 (reading candles) → Level 2 (The Trap & Snap strategy).
  • The 🔀 button shuffles the deck.

🎞️ Replay Engine — the important one

This is a time machine for practice. It shows you a real-feeling market morning, one candle at a time — but hides the future, exactly like live trading.

  • NEXT BAR ␣ (or spacebar on Mac) → shows the next candle. Keep tapping to move time forward.
  • Two dashed lines on the chart: green = the floor (premarket low), red = the ceiling (premarket high). Faded candles on the left are overnight; bright ones are today.
  • When price gets near the floor or ceiling, the app FREEZES and makes you write down your trade BEFORE showing you what happens next: direction, entry, bail (where you exit if wrong), target (where you cash out), and one sentence why. It will not let you skip this. That moment of "uh… what do I think?" is the whole point.
  • ✋ Skip / no trade = "I don't see a trade." Sometimes that's the RIGHT answer — some sessions are designed to have no setup, and skipping them scores as a win.
  • At the end it tells you the truth about the session, your R-multiple (how much you made vs. how much you risked — 2R means you made double your risk), and asks you 4 quick grade questions. Answer honestly, tap SAVE TO JOURNAL.
Beginner trap: don't get mad when it says "🛑 BAILED OUT." A bail is a small planned loss — that's the app working correctly. Pros bail all the time. What you're training is following the plan, not being right every time.

📓 The journal

Every session you finish gets saved at the bottom of the Replay page: WIN / LOSS / SKIP, your R-multiple, and ✓✗ marks for your process. It stays in your browser even when you close the app. The ⬇ Export button downloads it as a file (handy later when the AI coach reads your week).

📅 What to do every day (the whole routine)

  • Anytime, phone: 5 flashcards or one quiz category (~3 minutes)
  • Evening, Mac or phone: one ticker page — read the Recap, then Practice that day (~10 minutes)
  • Once a week: look at your journal and home-page progress — not "did I win?" but "did I follow the plan?" Following the plan while losing is progress. Winning while winging it is a warning.
The one rule above all rules: this app will never ask you for real money, and you shouldn't put real money into trading until your journal shows weeks of consistent, rule-following practice. The app is the gatekeeper — let it do its job.

🛠️ If something looks wrong

  • Page looks blank? Refresh. Everything reloads in seconds; your journal is safe.
  • Buttons not responding? Make sure you finished the current step (the Replay won't advance until you commit or skip — that's on purpose).
  • Lost your journal? It only disappears if you clear your browser data. Export it weekly if you want a backup.
  • Something confusing? That's a design bug, not a you problem. Screenshot it and tell Kimi "this confused me" — it gets rewritten.
