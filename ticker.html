    Practice — trade this real day one candle at a time

    You start at 10:00 AM (the opening 30 minutes set the floor & ceiling). Press Next candle (or spacebar) to move time forward. Buy or short when YOU see a setup. You'll be graded against the best trade of the day.

    Playground — “what if I got in here, out there?”

    Tap the chart twice: 1st tap = where you'd ENTER, 2nd tap = where you'd EXIT. Or pick times below. Then see exactly how much you would have won or lost. No money, no shame — just learning.
